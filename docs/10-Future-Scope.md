# 10. Future Scope

## Potential Enhancements
1. **Developer Skill Growth Tracking**: Automated skill proficiency increments based on verified completed task deliverables.
2. **Interactive Gantt Chart Visualizations**: Timeline-based workload distribution and project milestone dependencies.
3. **Calendar & Meeting Integrations**: Integration with Google Calendar / Outlook for fine-grained hourly bandwidth scheduling.
4. **Automated Notification Webhooks**: Slack/Microsoft Teams notifications when tasks are assigned to engineers.
5. **Team Workload Balancing Suggestions**: Automated alerts when a developer's weekly allocation exceeds 40 estimated hours.
